Open-source parts inside Reel Studio

Last updated October 30, 2026

Reel Studio uses open-source software, and each part keeps its own licence. This page is the source offer for the programs we build ourselves and ship in the installers: FFmpeg and FFprobe, plus the two libraries that open iPhone (HEIC) photos.

FFmpeg 7.1.1, build rs2 (GNU GPL v3)

Reel Studio runs ffmpeg and ffprobe as separate programs to read, convert and render video. We compile them from the unmodified FFmpeg 7.1.1 source with the libraries below. Because they include x264 and are built with --enable-gpl --enable-version3, the two programs are licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3. A copy of the licence ships next to them in the app.

The files below are the complete corresponding source for the FFmpeg and FFprobe programs shipped in the Reel Studio installers, for both Windows and macOS. We keep them online for as long as those builds are distributed. No account or purchase is needed to download them.

To rebuild: unpack the recipe and run bash build.sh <workdir> (in an MSYS2 UCRT64 shell on Windows, or a terminal on an Apple silicon Mac); its README lists the tools it needs. The script checks every source against the SHA-256 in sources.txt before it compiles. A rebuild gives an equivalent program, not necessarily a byte-identical file. You can also point Reel Studio at your own FFmpeg build.
