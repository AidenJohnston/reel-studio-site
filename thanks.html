2. Connect your own AI

Reel Studio's AI editor runs on your own paid Claude or ChatGPT plan. Setting it up takes three steps:

  1. Install and open Reel Studio.
  2. Click Connect Claude or Connect ChatGPT. If you don't have Anthropic's Claude Code app or OpenAI's Codex app yet, Reel Studio installs the official one for you.
  3. Sign in on the vendor's own page. Anthropic's or OpenAI's sign-in opens in your browser. Reel Studio never sees that sign-in, your password or any token.

The edits run on your own plan and count against its usage limits. You can also skip this and use Reel Studio without an AI: it still cuts the pauses, adds captions and music, and makes a cover.
